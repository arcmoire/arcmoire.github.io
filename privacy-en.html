Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

1.Introduction

Arc:moire (“the App”) is an app for iPhone and iPad that introduces you to three buildings and architects every day and helps you remember them. The App shows no ads and does not use any mechanism for usage analytics or tracking (third-party SDKs or advertising identifiers). Your information is not sold.

The App is made by an individual developer (“the Developer”) and provided free of charge. There is no account registration, and the Developer runs no servers. Your learning records and your location are kept on your device (and, when you sync with iCloud, your records also in your iCloud) and do not reach the Developer.

2.Information the Developer receives and how it is used

Only the following information reaches the Developer. All of it is used only to fix and improve the App.

2.1 Feedback form and email

  • The Developer receives what you write and any contact information you choose to include. Through the form, the Developer also receives the App version and your device (model identifier and iOS version).
  • This is used to fix bugs and errors in the App’s entries, to consider requests, and to reply if you included contact information.

2.2 Photo crop adjustments

  • If you send them with “Send Adjusted Photos” in Settings, the Developer receives the ID and name of each photo you adjusted, where each photo came from, the position and size values, and the App version and your device (model identifier and iOS version). You choose how to send them (Messages, LINE, etc.), and your name or account on that service also reaches the Developer.
  • This is used to improve how photos are shown. The position and size values may be incorporated into how photos appear in the App (your name and similar details are not incorporated).

2.3 Beta version (TestFlight)

  • For people who use the beta version, Apple provides the Developer with information such as your name, email address, crash logs and the feedback you send. For details, see Apple’s “TestFlight & Privacy”.
  • This is used to fix and improve the beta version.

2.4 Analytics data for the App Store version

  • If you have turned on Share With App Developers in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements on your iPhone, Apple provides the Developer with crash logs and usage statistics in a form that does not identify you personally.
  • This is used to find and fix bugs.
  • This data is kept in Apple’s developer dashboard (App Store Connect), and Apple decides how long it is kept there. Anything the Developer exports and keeps is deleted once the issue is resolved.

2.5 Who sees it and how it is protected

  • Only the Developer and people who help with development see this information. Those helpers are asked to use it only to improve the App and not to disclose it to anyone else.
  • The Google account behind the contact address is protected with 2-Step Verification. If you contact the Developer, the Developer will explain in more detail how this information is protected.

3.Information on your device and in your iCloud

The following information is stored on your device. The App does not send it to the Developer (photo crop adjustments reach the Developer only when you send them; see 2.2). When you sync with iCloud, a copy of your records is kept in your iCloud (see “iCloud sync” below).

  • Your progress (which buildings you have learned and how well) and the days you practiced
  • Buildings and architects you saved, lists you made, and the history of your daily three
  • Recent searches (up to 10) and settings
  • Marks for events you added to your calendar, photo crop adjustments, cached photos, and similar data
  • The last location obtained

This information may be included in your iPhone’s iCloud Backup and in backup files you create with “Export Records” in Settings.

  • iCloud sync: When “Sync with iCloud” is on in Settings (it is on from the start), a copy of your progress and the days you practiced, your saved items, the lists you made, the history of your daily three, and your study settings (Question Pool, Intensity, Questions per Session, Filters and notification time) is kept in the iCloud of your Apple Account (on Apple’s servers), so that they are the same on every iPhone and iPad signed in to the same Apple Account. The copy is in your own iCloud; the Developer can neither read nor see it. Recent searches, your location, photo crop adjustments and calendar marks are not included. Apple handles it under Apple’s privacy policy.
  • Location: With “While Using the App” permission, location is used only to find nearby architecture and to show your current location on the map. Distances and directions are calculated on your device. The last location is kept so that Siri can find nearby architecture, but it is not used after 24 hours and is deleted the next time you open the App.
  • Siri and Spotlight: Names of buildings and architects and similar information are provided to the Spotlight index and Siri Suggestions on your device. What you say to Siri is handled under Apple’s policies.
  • Apple Intelligence: Your search words are turned into search conditions by the language model on your device.
  • Visual Intelligence: What appears in your camera view or on your screen is compared, on your device, with the App’s photos. Images are neither saved nor sent.

4.Communication with outside services

The App communicates with outside services in the following cases. When it does, information needed for the communication, such as your IP address, reaches the other party.

  • Wikimedia (Commons and Wikidata)
    • When loading photos that are not bundled with the App (most building photos, architect portraits, and so on). Wikimedia receives which photo is being loaded.
    • When checking whether architects have died (at first launch, and after that once every 30 days; you can turn this off in Settings). Wikimedia receives the list of Wikidata IDs of the architects the App records as living.
    • In both cases, the App identifies itself with its name, its version and the contact email address.
  • Apple
    • When showing maps, and when you tap “Open in Maps.” On a building’s detail screen, the App looks up the building’s Apple Maps place ID with Apple to show opening hours and similar details.
    • When you use voice input. Your voice may be sent to Apple’s servers to be converted to text.
    • When you sync with iCloud. A copy of your records is sent to your iCloud (see “iCloud sync” in 3).
  • Google
    • When you open the feedback form, Google receives the App version and your device. When you submit it, Google receives what you wrote.

For how each company handles information, see the privacy policies of Wikimedia, Apple and Google. How outside sites that you open from links (such as Wikipedia) handle information depends on each site’s policies.

5.How long information is kept and how to delete it

  • Information that reaches the Developer (2) is deleted once it has been dealt with and the matter is resolved.
  • Information on your device (3) can be deleted within the App, and is also deleted when you delete the App.
  • The copy in iCloud is erased, together with the copies on your other syncing devices, when you use “Erase Progress” or “Erase History.” Saved items and lists are also removed from iCloud when you remove or delete them. To delete the whole copy, turn off “Sync with iCloud” in Settings and then tap “Delete iCloud Copy” (if another device is still syncing, it will put a copy back). Deleting the App does not delete the copy in iCloud.
  • You can withdraw permission for location, notifications, the microphone and speech recognition at any time in Settings › Apps › Arc:moire on your iPhone. The check for architects’ deaths can be turned off in the App’s settings.

6.Contact

Contact: arc.architecture.app@gmail.com

  • If you ask, the Developer will tell you the Developer’s name and address without delay.
  • If you are under 16, please get consent from your parent or guardian before writing your contact information.

7.Changes and effective date

  • If the information handled changes, this page will be updated and its effective date revised.
  • If the uses of collected information are expanded, you will be told in the App and asked for your consent first.
  • This policy takes effect on October 30, 2026.
